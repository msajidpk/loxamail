# Business

LoxaMail is a product and brand of ONLYZER, not a separate legal entity. Read the Customer Architecture and Product Direction sections of `LOXAMAIL_MASTER_SPEC.md` for the approved business model and catalogue boundaries. [Customer & Identity Architecture V1](customer-identity-v1.md) defines the conceptual Customer Account, Contact, User, membership, role, and access boundaries. [Commercial Model V1](commercial-model-v1.md) records product-independent commercial concepts and decisions that remain pending.

[Sales & Order Lifecycle V1](sales-order-lifecycle-v1.md) documents the conceptual direct and quote-based sales paths, order items, service creation, fulfillment, and unresolved workflow decisions.

[Service & Subscription Lifecycle V1](service-subscription-lifecycle-v1.md) documents purchased Service and recurring Subscription boundaries, lifecycle states, renewals, changes, and unresolved operational rules.

[Billing & Invoice Architecture V1](billing-invoice-v1.md) defines the conceptual billing records, invoice snapshot integrity, payment/credit/refund distinctions, and pending financial decisions.

[Support & Ticketing Architecture V1](support-ticketing-v1.md) defines Customer Account-owned support requests, communications, assignment, related-record context, and unresolved support policies.

[CRM & Sales Pipeline Architecture V1](crm-sales-pipeline-v1.md) documents internal Lead, Opportunity, Activity, pipeline, and sales handoff concepts while preserving separate Customer Account, Quote/Order, Support, Billing, and Product modules.

[Vendor & Purchasing Architecture V1](vendor-purchasing-v1.md) defines internal Vendor/Supplier, Supplier Offering, purchase, supplier-cost, Vendor Invoice, and Vendor Payment concepts separately from customer records and billing.

[Documents & Notifications Architecture V1](documents-notifications-v1.md) defines reusable cross-platform Documents and Notifications, storage and delivery-channel boundaries, and customer/internal access concepts without selecting providers or policies.
